ReelGen · Asset workflow

From your assets to a finished reel.

Upload the image ZIP and storyboard JSON you already have. ReelGen validates the scenes, renders locally with your configured voice and caption pipeline, then lets you publish to selected connected accounts.

⌂ Runs on this computer

01 / Upload your production files

Choose script.json with a non-empty scenes array and a ZIP containing the referenced PNG/JPG/WEBP illustrations. Images are copied into a local workspace; unsafe ZIP paths are rejected.

Uses the local Python pipeline and TTS backend configured in .env.

02 / Review the finished video

Preview the render and check its platform copy before posting.

↓ Download MP4Open review page ↗

03 / Publish to your social accounts

Select destinations and publish from one button. Each destination reports success or failure independently; already-published destinations are skipped to prevent duplicate posts.

Publishing can make content public. TikTok posting is not yet implemented here; its video and caption are provided for manual upload. Instagram, Facebook and X depend on account type, API permissions and platform limits.

Account setup

Connect once, then publish from this page. Secrets and OAuth tokens stay in local .env/data files and are never displayed here.

Instagram requires an access token and business account ID in .env. Connect Drive staging only authorizes temporary media staging; it does not authorize Instagram itself.
